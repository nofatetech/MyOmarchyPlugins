# Open questions

- Own kids only, a classroom, or something to distribute later?
- Kids' exact ages and existing interests (seed the first project).
- School, homeschool, or out-of-school time only?
- Country/region (Germany? affects internship and Ausbildung pathways).

## Trust and safety
- Internet: open, filtered, or curated?
- Which activities need a person present, which only need reminders?
- Who the mentors are today (family, neighbors, makerspace)?

## Product
- AI helper: none, "how do I do X" helper, or coding buddy?
- Kid "OS skin" or Omarchy with training wheels?
- One profile per kid with level toggle, or shared?
- Which skill/project first (3D print, soldering kit, Arduino, game)?

## Hardware
- What workshop gear already exists (printer, soldering station, DMX, audio)?
- Where does the computer live: fixed station, cart, travelling laptop?
- Power and network in the garage/treehouse.

## Technical
- Name of the project.
- Qt app vs local web app for the hub and link saver.
- Sync and sharing approach (Syncthing, local Git, something else).
